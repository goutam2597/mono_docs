MONO Marketplace: Key Features

  • Platform Owner & Admin Panel Highlights
    • Multi-Country Marketplace Management: Easily launch and manage marketplaces across multiple countries with dedicated currencies, languages, tax settings, and localized shopping experiences.
    • Category-Based Commission System: Configure different commission rates for each product category and effortlessly manage vendor earnings and payout records.
    • Warehouse & Fulfillment Management: Manage warehouses, fulfillment centers, and vendor inventory locations to streamline order processing and shipping operations.
    • Comprehensive Data & Reporting: Access detailed insights into marketplace performance, including sales trends, top vendors, popular products, customer growth, and revenue reports.
  • Customer App Highlights
    • Global Shopping Experience: Customers can browse products, discover brands, and shop seamlessly from vendors operating across multiple cities and countries.
    • Dedicated Vendor Storefronts: Each vendor gets a branded storefront where customers can explore products, collections, ratings, and business information.
    • Smart Product Discovery: Advanced search, filters, categories, and recommendations help customers quickly find exactly what they need.
    • Wishlist & Save for Later: Allow customers to save favorite products, organize collections, and conveniently reorder items anytime.
    • Secure Checkout & Multiple Payment Gateways: Provide a seamless checkout experience with support for multiple payment methods and trusted payment gateways.
  • Vendor/Vendor Panel Highlights
    • Independent Store Management: Vendors can customize stores, upload banners, manage business information, and establish their own brand presence.
    • Powerful Catalog Management: Easily manage products, variants, attributes, specifications, media galleries, and SEO settings through an intuitive dashboard.
    • Inventory & Warehouse Control: Track stock levels, manage inventory across one or multiple warehouses, and maintain accurate product availability.
    • Order Processing & Fulfillment: Accept orders, update statuses, generate invoices, and efficiently manage shipping operations.
    • Sales Insights & Performance Reports: Monitor sales, earnings, best-selling products, customer activity, and overall store performance through detailed reports.
  • Marketplace Highlights
    • Multi-Vendor Marketplace Architecture: Empower multiple vendors to operate independent businesses while maintaining complete control over marketplace operations.
    • Shipping Profiles & Shipping Zones: Configure shipping methods, delivery estimates, shipping costs, and supported regions for local and international deliveries.
    • Multi-Currency & Multi-Language Support: Deliver localized shopping experiences with support for multiple currencies and languages tailored to each market.
    • Product Reviews & Ratings: Build customer trust by enabling verified reviews, ratings, and vendor feedback.
    • Coupons & Promotional Campaigns: Boost sales with discount campaigns, coupons, featured products, and seasonal promotions.
  • Server Requirements
    • PHP Version: PHP 8.4+ (compatible with newer PHP versions).
    • Flutter SDK: Flutter SDK 3.x.x (compatible with newer stable Flutter releases).
    • Database: MySQL 8.0+ (or compatible newer versions).

Additional Service Costs

Some third-party services used by MONO Marketplace require separate subscriptions or usage-based payments and are not included with the purchase.

  • Payment Gateways: Charges imposed by payment providers such as Stripe, Razorpay, and other supported payment processors.
  • SMS Notification Providers: Costs associated with sending SMS messages through services like Twilio, MSG91, Vonage, or similar providers.
  • Email Services: Transactional email delivery services such as SMTP providers, SendGrid, Mailgun, Amazon SES, and similar platforms may require separate subscriptions.
  • Maps & Location Services: Usage charges for Google Maps Platform, Mapbox, or other mapping and geolocation APIs.
  • App Store Developer Accounts: Publishing mobile apps requires active developer accounts such as the Google Play Developer Account and Apple Developer Program, which involve separate registration or annual fees.

Note: Any fees, subscriptions, or usage charges imposed by third-party platforms or service providers are not included in the purchase price of MONO Marketplace and are the sole responsibility of the buyer.
