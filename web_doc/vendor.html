01 / Overview

One codebase, three front doors

MONO Marketplace is a multi-vendor commerce platform. A single Laravel application serves three completely separate front doors, and they all share one database, one permission system, and one theme engine.

STOREFRONT

Customer website

  • Themeable catalog with nine shipped storefront themes.
  • Cart, coupons, vouchers, wishlist, loyalty, and wallet.
  • Fourteen payment methods including cash on delivery and bank transfer.
  • Digital goods, bundles, and vendor chat with realtime delivery.
View Storefront ↗
VENDOR PANEL

Seller dashboard

  • Applications, product, bundle, and taxonomy management.
  • Order fulfilment with per-item digital delivery.
  • Settlements, withdrawal requests, subscription packages.
  • AI suite with metered text, translation, and image quotas.
View Live Vendor Panel ↗
ADMIN PANEL

Platform control

  • Orders, invoices, refunds, and a full finance ledger.
  • Vendor approvals, subscriptions, payouts, and delivery operations.
  • Roles, permissions, languages, media, and site settings.
  • Push broadcasting, newsletters, and an AI administration toolset.
View Live Admin Panel ↗

What ships in the package

Storefront themes

Nine themes on disk: mono (built in), plus beauty, electronics, fashion, fullwidth, grocery, home, petshop, and sports. Each is a self-contained package with its own views, assets, palette, and optional database tables.

Mobile applications

Two Flutter clients ship alongside: the customer app and the vendor app. Both talk to the same versioned API described in this document.

Web installer

public/install.php is a self-contained wizard that creates the database, writes the environment file, creates the first administrator, and activates a theme. No Composer, Node, or shell access is required on the host.

The three front doors, at a glance

Each interface is purpose-built for desktop web workflows — responsive Blade templates styled with Tailwind CSS, with zero client-side compilation steps.

Who this documentation is for

This guide covers the web product: the storefront, the vendor panel, the admin panel, and the backend services that support them. The two Flutter applications have their own companion documents. Everything here is written against the shipped source, so route names, table names, and permission keys match what you will find in the package.

13 / Vendor panel

Vendor dashboard and store operations

Live Vendor Panel Demo ↗

The vendor portal is served from /dashboard (on your domain: https://your-domain.com/vendor) behind the auth:vendor guard, vendor.verified, and vendor.approved middleware. Each vendor operates inside an isolated tenancy boundary: every query is scoped to vendor_id, so merchants only ever see and manage their own store catalog, orders, media, and finances.

Dashboard metrics

The landing page provides immediate operational visibility for merchants:

  • Net Earnings (30d): Net income from paid, fulfilled items minus marketplace commission and refunds.
  • Order Volume: Total order count with monthly delta comparisons.
  • Average Order Value (AOV): Average transaction spend across store orders.
  • Live Products: Published catalog count with inventory growth metrics.
  • AI Usage & Quotas: Real-time consumption meter for text generation tokens, translation tokens, and generated product imagery. Displays clear status indicators when allowances are reached.
  • 12-Week Revenue Trajectory: Rolling financial bar chart plotting sales momentum.
  • Top Products Leaderboard: Top 5 revenue-generating items in the vendor's catalog.

14 / Vendor panel

Products, digital fulfilment, settlements, and AI

Vendors manage their complete ecommerce lifecycle from the web dashboard, supporting physical goods with complex variations as well as instantly deliverable digital goods.

Catalog and variations

Product creation in DashboardController handles both simple and variable products. Variable products synchronize attribute combinations (sizes, colors, fabrics) using VariationSyncService:

  • Each variation has its own SKU, inventory stock, regular price, and sale price.
  • Dynamic matrix creation generates all valid permutations without manual database configuration.
  • Vendors can request new categories (/dashboard/categories/request) or register custom brand names (/dashboard/brands) subject to admin review.

Product bundles

The bundle editor at /dashboard/product-bundles lets vendors group complementary products into discounted bundle packages. ProductBundleController validates inventory across all bundle items and ensures accurate pricing on checkout.

Digital goods & instant delivery

Products marked is_digital support downloadable files, license keys, and digital entitlements. When an order is placed and paid:

  • The vendor accesses /dashboard/orders/{order} and triggers orders.digital-deliver.
  • The system mints a DigitalEntitlement record with access limits and secure download tokens.
  • The customer immediately receives download access in their account library and via email notification.

Settlements and withdrawals

Every completed sale generates a settlement row calculating the exact revenue breakdown:

Settlement Equation: Net Vendor Payout = Item Subtotal - Platform Commission + Tax (if vendor-collected)

The settlements table at /dashboard/settlements tracks pending, available, and paid earnings. Vendors request payouts via /dashboard/withdrawals once their balance satisfies the minimum withdrawal threshold configured in platform settings.

Featured sponsorships

Vendors can promote individual products to prime homepage and category slots via /dashboard/products/{product}/feature. The vendor chooses a featured duration package and completes payment directly in the panel using Stripe, SSLCommerz, or PayPal. Upon payment verification, the product is flagged is_featured = 1 until the expiration timestamp.

Vendor AI suite

The dashboard incorporates integrated AI tooling powered by OpenAI / Google Gemini providers:

  • Product Description Writer: Generates compelling ecommerce copy and bullet points from minimal attribute inputs.
  • Multilingual Translation: 1-click translation of titles and descriptions across English, Arabic, and Bengali.
  • Credit Packs: When monthly package quotas are exhausted, merchants purchase non-expiring AI credit packs directly through /dashboard/ai-credits.

15 / Vendor panel

Becoming a vendor: from sign-up to first sale

New merchants join without any admin-side work: registration, document verification, and approval are all handled through the storefront and the vendor portal. This is the exact path a new vendor walks:

  1. Open a store.

    On the vendor portal sign-in page (your-domain.com/vendor), click Open a store. The multi-step registration asks for the store name, contact details, and the owner's account credentials.

  2. Submit verification documents (KYC).

    Depending on the platform's onboarding settings, the vendor uploads business/trade documents during registration or from the dashboard. The store enters the verified pending state (vendor.verified).

  3. Wait for admin approval.

    The marketplace owner reviews the application under Admin → Vendors and approves the store (vendor.approved). Until both checks pass, the auth:vendor guard refuses dashboard access — a rejected vendor sees the exact reason on the sign-in screen.

  4. Sign in and land on the overview.

    After approval, sign in at your-domain.com/vendor; the dashboard at /dashboard opens with net earnings, order volume, live products, and the AI quota meter.

  5. Complete the store profile.

    Open Settings (/dashboard/settings) before adding products: upload the store logo, fill the multilingual store name and description, contact email/phone, category, and full address, then set the payout method under the Payouts tab so settlements have somewhere to go.

  6. Add products and go live.

    Create the catalog (next section), keep everything within the package listing quota, and publish. Products appear on the storefront immediately.

Trying the demo? The vendor sign-in page shows a Demo vendor login box with copy-and-autofill credentials. Demo mode disables write actions — you can look around every screen but nothing you click changes data.

Customer engagement

Inbox — real-time chat

/dashboard/messages is the vendor side of the Ably-powered chat: answer pre-sale questions, negotiate, and share files. Conversations open from orders and product pages on the storefront.

Followers & broadcasts

/dashboard/followers lists the customers following your store and lets you start broadcast conversations — the direct channel for announcements and offers.

Money & growth

Settlements & withdrawals

/dashboard/settlements itemizes every sale (subtotal − commission + tax) across pending, available, and paid balances; /dashboard/withdrawals requests payouts once the platform minimum is met. Configure where the money goes under Settings → Payouts.

Packages & AI credits

/dashboard/packages shows your membership tier — listing quotas and commission discounts — and offers upgrades. /dashboard/ai-credits buys non-expiring credit packs when the monthly AI allowance runs out.

Store settings reference

/dashboard/settings has two tabs. Store profile: logo (choose from the media library or remove), store name and description in English, Arabic, and Bengali, store URL slug, contact email and phone, business category, and full location (street, country, state, city, postal code). Payouts: payout method and account details used for withdrawals. Save changes applies to the storefront immediately.
